Symptoms We Treat

Does this sound familiar?

TMJ trouble rarely stays in the jaw. When the muscles that open and close your mouth stay tight, the strain shows up in your head, your face and your sleep. Emface works on that muscular side of the problem.

Jaw pain & aching

A dull ache along the jaw or just in front of the ear, often worse after chewing or a long day.

Clenching & grinding

Teeth pressed together through the day or ground at night, leaving the jaw sore by morning.

Tension headaches

Pressure that starts at the temples and spreads, driven by overworked chewing muscles.

Muscle tightness & spasm

Chewing muscles that feel knotted, tender to the touch, or that tighten up without warning.

Limited jaw movement

Trouble opening wide to yawn, take a bite, or sit comfortably through a dental visit.

Face, neck & ear soreness

Tenderness that radiates into the cheeks, neck or around the ear, where the jaw muscles attach.

Warmth and movement, working in sync

Emface pairs synchronized radiofrequency with HIFES muscle stimulation. The heat helps tight, spasming jaw muscles let go, and the stimulation retrains them to move more freely. Hands-free, in about 20 minutes.

Emface for TMJ, explained

Your temporomandibular joints connect the lower jaw to the skull, just in front of each ear, and a group of chewing muscles moves them every time you talk, eat or yawn. When those joints or muscles are strained, often by clenching, grinding or stress, you can feel jaw pain, tight or tender muscles, headaches, clicking, or trouble opening wide.

Emface is FDA-cleared to treat TMJ dysfunction and associated pain. Its radiofrequency raises tissue temperature to relieve symptoms of muscle spasm and improve local circulation. Its HIFES stimulation activates the jaw muscles to help ease pain and maintain or increase your range of motion. Both work at the same time during each session.

A night guard protects your teeth from grinding while you sleep, and Botox quiets an overactive muscle with an injection. Emface takes a different route, warming and working the muscle so it can relax and move better, with no needle and no medication. Some patients use it on its own and others alongside a night guard. Dr. Nory will tell you honestly which approach makes sense for you.

Most patients describe gentle muscle contractions combined with a warming sensation, similar to a relaxing facial massage. It is hands-free, you simply lie back for about 20 minutes, and you can go straight back to your day with zero downtime.

A typical course is four 20-minute sessions spaced about a week apart, though Dr. Nory may tailor the plan to your symptoms. Most patients notice changes after completing the series, and some feel relief after the very first session.

Emface may not be suitable if you have metal implants in the treatment area, an implanted electronic medical device such as a pacemaker, a history of seizures, or if you are pregnant or breastfeeding. Jaw pain can also have causes that need a different kind of care, which is why every plan starts with an exam and a short consultation.
